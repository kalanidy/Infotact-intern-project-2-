# 📊 Customer Retention & CLTV Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze customer retention patterns, Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV), revenue trends, and customer purchasing behavior using the Online Retail dataset.

## 📌 Project Overview

This dashboard provides business insights into customer retention, revenue generation, and customer lifetime value. It helps identify high-value customers, top-performing products, and revenue contributions across different countries.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing behavior
- Measure Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV)
- Monitor customer retention trends
- Identify top revenue-generating customers
- Analyze country-wise revenue distribution
- Discover best-selling products

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### KPI Cards

- 💰 Total Revenue
- 👥 Total Customers
- 🛒 Total Orders
- 💳 Average Order Value
- 💎 Average CLTV
- 🔄 Repeat Customers

---

### Visualizations

- 📈 Monthly Revenue Trend
- 🌍 Revenue by Country
- 📉 Customer Retention Decay
- 🏆 Top 10 Customers by Revenue
- 💎 CLTV by Country
- 📦 Top 10 Products by Revenue

### Interactive Filters

- Country
- Product Description

## 📂 Dataset

**Dataset:** Online Retail Dataset

Contains transactional sales data including:

- Customer ID
- Invoice Date
- Product Description
- Country
- Quantity
- Unit Price

## 📌 Key Business Insights

- Generated over **$3.44M** in total revenue.
- United Kingdom contributes the highest revenue.
- Customer retention decreases after the first purchase cycle.
- Repeat customers generate a significant portion of revenue.
- Top customers and products contribute a major share of sales.
